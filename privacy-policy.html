Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Winnipeg Fence Installation ("we," "us," or "our") operates winnipegfenceinstallation.ca (the "Site"). This page explains what information we collect when you use the Site, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you fill out a contact or quote request form, including your name, phone number, email address, and any details about your fencing project.

Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics and/or Google Ads to understand how visitors use the Site and to measure the performance of our advertising. These services use cookies to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior information in an anonymous form. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your quote request or inquiry
  • Connect you with a service provider matching your request
  • Improve the Site and our services
  • Send you information relevant to your request, when applicable

How We Share Your Information

When you submit a quote request, we share your name, phone number, email address, and project details with the independent service provider(s) we believe are best suited to fulfill your request. We receive a referral fee or commission from these providers in exchange for connecting them with your request — this is how we're able to offer this service to homeowners at no cost. We do not sell or share your information with unrelated third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention

We retain the information you submit for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law.

Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and request corrections. To do so, contact us using the information below.

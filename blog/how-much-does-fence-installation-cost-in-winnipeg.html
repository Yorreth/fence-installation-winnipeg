How Much Does Fence Installation Cost in Winnipeg? (2026 Price Guide)

Fence pricing swings more than most home projects because so much depends on material, total length, and what's already on the property. Here's a realistic breakdown by fence type, based on typical Winnipeg installed pricing.

Typical installed cost by material (per linear foot)

Fence typeTypical range

Commercial/industrialVaries by scope — quote required

For a typical 100–150 ft residential backyard, that translates to roughly $2,000–$6,000+ depending on material and height. These are general ranges — your actual quote depends on the specifics below.

What actually drives the price up

  • Old fence removal. Tearing out and hauling away an existing fence adds labour before the new one even starts.
  • Height. Taller fences need more material and, often, larger posts and footings.
  • Corners and gates. Every corner post and gate takes more time and hardware than a straight run.
  • Ground conditions. Rocky or root-filled soil, or an unusually deep frost line in a given yard, can slow post digging.
  • Access. Backyards only reachable through a narrow side gate may limit equipment and add labour time.
Be cautious of a quote that's dramatically cheaper than everyone else's — the most common way installers cut cost is by setting posts shallower or using less concrete than the frost line requires. That's the repair bill waiting to happen two or three winters later.

Getting a fair quote

The only way to get an exact number is a quick, free property assessment — total footage, gate count, and any removal needed all factor in. Ask every quote you get how deep posts will be set and what backfill is included; it's the easiest way to compare apples to apples.

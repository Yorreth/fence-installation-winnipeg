What to plan for on a commercial job

Larger runs of fencing mean more posts, more concrete, and more coordination — especially around underground utilities, which need to be located before any digging starts. Commercial properties may also have specific insurance, zoning, or municipal requirements depending on use and location; your matched pro can help identify what applies before work begins.

Frost heave doesn't care whether a fence is residential or commercial — longer commercial runs actually multiply the cost of getting post depth wrong, since every post that fails means a section of fence line to fix. For larger jobs, ask about the post spacing and footing spec in writing before work starts.

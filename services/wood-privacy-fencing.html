Wood Privacy Fencing

Wood Privacy Fence Installation in Winnipeg

A wood privacy fence is still the most requested fence type for Winnipeg backyards — it blocks sightlines from neighbours, muffles street noise, and gives dogs and kids a contained yard without the industrial look of chain link. The tradeoff is that wood is the material most sensitive to how well it's installed, especially in a city with soil and winters like ours.

Cedar vs. pressure-treated spruce/pine

  • Cedar naturally resists rot and insects, ages to a soft grey, and holds up well without staining — it costs more upfront.
  • Pressure-treated spruce/pine/fir (SPF) is the budget-friendly standard, chemically treated to resist rot; it needs staining or sealing every few years to look its best and last.

Why post depth matters more here than most places

Winnipeg sits on heavy clay soil often nicknamed "gumbo," and the frost line runs deep. If fence posts aren't set below that frost depth — or don't have enough concrete around them — the ground's freeze-thaw cycle will push them upward over a few winters. That's the single most common reason a Winnipeg wood fence starts leaning or gapping at the bottom within a few years of installation.

A properly built fence uses posts set well below the local frost depth, backfilled with concrete or a compacted gravel base, not just dirt tamped back into the hole. Ask your matched pro how deep they're setting posts — it's the detail that determines whether your fence lasts 5 years or 25.

What's typically included

  1. Layout & property line check. Confirming the fence line, any setback rules for your yard, and corner-lot sightline restrictions if it applies.
  2. Post setting. Holes dug below frost depth, posts set plumb and backfilled with concrete.
  3. Rail and panel installation. Horizontal rails go up first, then vertical boards or pre-built panels are attached.
  4. Gate(s) and hardware. Hinges and latches sized for the gate weight, adjusted so it swings and latches cleanly.

What it costs

Wood privacy fencing typically runs in the $30–$55 per linear foot range installed in Winnipeg, depending on height, wood grade, and whether an old fence needs to be torn out first. The only way to get an exact number is a quick, free assessment of your yard.

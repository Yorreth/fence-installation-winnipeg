Residential Fence Installation in Winnipeg

If you know you want a fence but aren't sure which material fits your property, budget, and the way you actually use your yard, this is the place to start. We match homeowners with local fencing pros who walk through the whole decision with you — not just the install.

What a full-service fence project involves

  1. Purpose first. Privacy, pet containment, pool safety, curb appeal, or a boundary marker each point toward different materials and heights.
  2. Property line confirmation. Especially for shared or disputed boundaries, confirming the line before digging saves headaches later.
  3. Material selection. Wood, chain link, vinyl/composite, or a mix, weighed against upfront cost, maintenance, and how long you plan to stay in the home.
  4. Design details. Height, gate placement and count, and whether style needs to match an existing fence on an adjoining property.
  5. Installation. Posts set below Winnipeg's frost line, panels or fabric installed, gates hung and adjusted.

Whatever material you choose, ask how deep the posts will be set and what they'll be backfilled with. That single spec matters more to how long your fence lasts than the material itself — Winnipeg's clay soil and frost depth are unforgiving toward shortcuts.

What it costs

Residential fence pricing depends on material, height, total length, and site prep — typical installed pricing ranges from roughly $18 per linear foot for basic chain link up to $80+ for premium vinyl or cedar. The only way to get an exact number for your yard is a quick, free assessment.

Fence Repair & Gate Installation in Winnipeg

Not every fence problem means starting over. Leaning posts, sagging gates, and storm-damaged panels can usually be fixed for a fraction of full replacement cost — as long as the rest of the fence structure is sound.

Common repairs we match homeowners with pros for

  • Frost-heaved or leaning posts — the most common Winnipeg fence issue, caused by posts set too shallow or without enough concrete
  • Sagging or dragging gates — often a hinge, post, or hardware fix rather than a full gate replacement
  • Storm and wind damage — broken panels, snapped pickets, or fabric torn loose from high winds
  • Rot and insect damage at the base of wood posts and boards
  • Rusted or bent chain link sections and fittings

Repair or replace?

A good rule of thumb: if the posts are solid and only a section of panel, fabric, or hardware has failed, repair is usually the cheaper and faster option. If multiple posts are heaving or leaning throughout the fence line, that's often a sign the whole fence was under-built to begin with, and replacement may be the better long-term investment. Mention what you're seeing when you request a quote and your matched pro can advise honestly on which route makes sense.

A single leaning post is often fixable by resetting it deeper with fresh concrete. If several posts along the same fence line are leaning the same direction, it usually points to a systemic issue with how the original fence was built — worth a full assessment before spending on a partial fix.

Adding a new gate

Whether you're adding side-yard access, a wider gate for equipment or a trailer, or simply replacing hardware that's worn out, gate installation follows the same frost-depth rules as the rest of the fence — gate posts take the most stress of any post on the fence line and need to be set solidly.

What it costs

Simple repairs like resetting a post or fixing hardware often run in the $150–$400 range, while larger repairs involving multiple posts or panel replacement cost more depending on scope. New gate installation typically runs $250–$800+ depending on size and material. A quick assessment gets you an exact number.

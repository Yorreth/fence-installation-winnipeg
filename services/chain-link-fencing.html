Chain Link Fencing

Chain Link Fence Installation in Winnipeg

Chain link is the fence homeowners choose when they want a solid boundary without spending on privacy panels — it's fast to install, low-maintenance for decades, and works well for side yards, dog runs, and rental properties where budget matters more than curb appeal.

Galvanized vs. vinyl-coated

  • Galvanized steel is the standard, budget option — zinc-coated to resist rust, holds up for years with essentially zero maintenance.
  • Vinyl-coated adds a black or green polymer coating over the steel, which resists scuffing and gives a slightly more finished look — a common choice for front yards or properties visible from the street.

Where chain link makes the most sense

  • Containing pets without blocking light or airflow
  • Rental and multi-unit properties that need a durable, low-upkeep boundary
  • Side and back yards where privacy isn't the priority
  • Pool code compliance fencing (check local pool enclosure requirements)
  • Temporary or semi-permanent site boundaries
Even a "low-maintenance" fence still needs posts set properly. Chain link posts set in shallow holes or without enough concrete will lean the same way wood posts do once Winnipeg's clay soil starts its winter freeze-thaw cycle — the fabric itself doesn't fail, the posts do.

What's typically included

  1. Post layout. Terminal (end/corner) posts and line posts spaced per the fence height and local frost depth.
  2. Post setting. Concrete footings, set plumb and left to cure before fabric goes up.
  3. Top rail & fabric installation. Mesh is stretched and tensioned, then clipped to the rail and posts.
  4. Gates. Walk gates and drive gates sized and hung to your access needs.

What it costs

Chain link is typically the most affordable fencing option in Winnipeg, often running $18–$35 per linear foot installed depending on height and gauge. Vinyl-coated fabric and taller heights run toward the top of that range.
